GETTING STARTED · VERSION 1.1

One piece is
a good beginning.

You don’t need to organize every lesson at once. Start with the piece you want to practice today.

  1. Create this week’s practice.

    On Home, tap New beside Your practices. Give the practice a name, choose the target week, and tap Create practice. You can start blank or copy an existing practice.

    Copying keeps pieces, score photos and written notes. It starts fresh practice totals and does not copy recordings.

  2. Add a piece and its score.

    Open the practice and tap Edit, then Add piece. Open the piece, enter its name, then tap Add score photos. Choose photos from your library or use the camera.

    Tap Save when your changes are ready.

  3. Leave a reminder at the right spot.

    Open a score photo and long-press the passage you want to remember. Choose a preset such as Rhythm or Fingering, add your own note, and save it. Tap a numbered marker to read or edit a note.

  4. Start practicing. Record if useful.

    Tap Start practice to run the practice timer. Audio recording has its own Record button. When you’re done, use End practice for the timer and Stop & save for an active recording.

    Pausing or ending practice does not stop an active recording. Check both controls before you finish.

Prefer to practice without recording?

Auto-record is off by default. If you’ve enabled it, turn it off in Settings before starting a new session. Changing that setting does not stop an already active recording.

Make it feel familiar.

In Settings, choose English, Simplified Chinese, or Follow system for the app language. System permission prompts follow your iPhone or iPad settings.
