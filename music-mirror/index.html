01 / PLAN

Bring the week together.

Create a weekly practice and add the pieces you’re working on. Keep score photos alongside each piece.

02 / REMEMBER

Put the reminder on the score.

Add your own notes at a specific spot. Keep a fingering reminder or a tricky rhythm close to the passage.

03 / PRACTICE

Make room for another try.

Track practice time with the timer. Record audio when it helps, and listen back to a saved take.

Remember what to work on.

Long-press a score photo to place a note. Choose a preset or write a reminder in your own words.
