A few useful answers.

New here? Start with the first-practice guide.

Can we practice without recording?

Yes. The practice timer works independently of audio recording. Auto-record is off by default; leave it off in Settings for new sessions without automatic recording. Use Record when you want an audio take.

Does End practice also stop the recording?

No. Pause and End practice control the timer. Stop & save controls an active recording. Check both controls when you finish. Practice time and recorded time are shown separately.

What happens when the screen locks or a call interrupts?

Locking the screen keeps the timer running; recording can also continue. Unlock and tap Stop & save when finished recording. Calls or Siri can interrupt a take. The app pauses practice when it receives an audio interruption; without recording, pause manually for calls or breaks.

How do I add a note to the score?

Open a score photo and long-press the spot you want to mark. Choose a preset or enter your own note, then save. Tap a numbered marker to read or edit it. These are notes you enter yourself; the app does not automatically detect playing mistakes.

Can I reuse last week’s pieces?

Yes. When creating a practice, choose an existing practice under Copy From. Piece order, score photos and written notes carry over. Recordings, voice notes and reference media do not; practice totals start at zero.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Music Mirror is free for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18.5 or later. Choose English, Simplified Chinese, or Follow system in Settings. See the App Store listing for current availability.

Do I need an account? Where is practice saved?

No account is required. Practices and recordings are stored on your device. Read the privacy policy for information about local data and external content. Contact support before changing devices or deleting the app if you need help preserving practice material.

The microphone or camera is not working. What can I do?

Check Music Mirror’s microphone or camera permission in your device’s Settings. Recording needs microphone access; taking a score photo needs camera access. You can still use the practice timer without recording, or select an existing score photo instead of using the camera. If the problem continues, contact support.

How can I report a problem or suggest something?

Email musicmirror@xinyuzhang.me. The support page has a short checklist to help describe what happened. You choose what to include and send.
